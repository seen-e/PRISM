# PRISM

**Precision Forcing for Generalist Vision-Language-Action Policies**

[Project Page](https://seen-e.github.io/PRISM/) · [Paper](https://seen-e.github.io/PRISM/assets/paper/PRISM.pdf) · [Videos](https://seen-e.github.io/PRISM/#experiments) · [BibTeX](https://seen-e.github.io/PRISM/assets/paper/prism.bib)

PRISM aligns policy learning with context-dependent precision demand for fine manipulation. Proprioceptive Precision Scoring (PPS) derives a training signal from demonstration trajectories, which guides supervision weighting and representation learning without changing the policy’s standard inference procedure.

The project page presents real-robot experiments in drive connector insertion, bimanual connector mating, and screw tightening, alongside perturbation examples and PPS visualizations from human VR teleoperation.

This repository hosts the project website, paper, and demonstration videos.
